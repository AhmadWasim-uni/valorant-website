About The Game

Tactical Gameplay

Valorant is a free-to-play first-person tactical hero shooter developed and published by Riot Games. Players compete in 5v5 matches where precise gunplay matters just as much as strategic ability usage.

Agents & Abilities

Players step into the shoes of Agents, each originating from different cultures and locations around the world. Every Agent has a unique set of abilities—including a powerful ultimate—designed to gather information, lock down sites, or breach enemy defenses.

The Arsenal

Matches are governed by an in-game economy. Players earn credits based on round outcomes, kills, and objectives, which they use to purchase a variety of weapons ranging from sidearms and SMGs to sniper rifles and heavy machine guns.
